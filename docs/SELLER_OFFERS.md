# Seller Offers and Vouchers

Seller promotions reuse the existing seller-offer reservations and points ledger and remain separate from administrator-created loyalty offers. RonTab advertises benefits and issues vouchers; it does not handle checkout, payment, ordering, delivery or returns.

## Product offers

- An active seller selects **Offer to the public** on an approved product. Existing product media, description and external link are reused; no second upload is required. Offers appear in `/visitor/offers` and as compact previews directly below points on `/visitor`.
- Public offers are visible to all eligible customers; exclusive rewards are limited to RonTab loyalty members. Both can require points for a branch voucher. The default is **100 customer points** for new branch vouchers, regardless of audience. Administrators can set this default and change an individual active voucher cost in `/admin/seller-offers`, using whole numbers from **50 to 1,000,000**. Online promotions use the approved product's HTTPS seller link and optional public code; they remain public, cost no points and have no voucher, usage tracking or enforceable quantity claim on external websites. Points are deducted from the customer's RonTab balance when reserving, never from the seller. This is not a cash conversion.
- Sellers can target up to **10 enabled UK cities**, with eligible customer counts alongside each city, or leave all cities unselected for **All UK**. All UK is the initial selection and includes supported UK cities enabled later, without duplicating branch or quota records. All cities use the same declared redemption postcode. Daily and total voucher quantities are shared, not multiplied; the gallery shows shared remaining daily availability. Online public offers support the same targeting without vouchers or quotas.
- Multi-city targeting requires `20261019210000_product_offer_multiple_cities.sql` and `20261019220000_all_uk_offers_and_admin_reward_points.sql`. `20261019250000_public_offer_points_cost.sql` separates audience from point cost, applies the configured default to active public branch offers and new branch vouchers, and keeps online offers free. Until the required migrations are applied, new targeting submissions fail without publishing a partial offer; existing city-specific discovery retains its compatibility path. Reordered or repeated city selections do not create duplicate offers. The atomic price-confirmation reservation RPC is supplied by the second migration.
- Changing the default does not reprice existing offers. The audience-and-cost migration updates active, auto-published, zero-cost branch offers to the configured default; existing vouchers retain their original spending and refund amount. Repricing an individual offer affects new reservations only. Publication retries never reset an administrator-adjusted price. Changes are recorded in the administrator audit log.
- Choose a percentage discount, free product, free meal or special benefit, with explicit details and exclusions. The seller accepts responsibility to honor it, including exclusivity for points rewards.
- Product offers publish automatically only for an active seller's own approved product with media, an enabled UK city, explicit terms acceptance and dates spanning at most 31 calendar days. A seller can have at most 10 current published/paused offers. Branch offers require a public redemption postcode, UK-local hours and daily/total quantities of 1 to 1,000. The total limit covers all dates, including expired unused reservations; cancelling a reservation releases its place.
- Automatic publication is recorded separately from administrator approval; it never attributes an administrative decision to the seller. Administration retains pause, resume and end controls. Retrying an identical publication key returns the original offer; changed payloads are rejected.
- The existing reviewed-request form remains available under **Request a reviewed offer** for special cases and historical drafts. Sellers submit a draft with UK branches, weekly schedule, requested daily capacities and media. Submitted requests cannot change without an administrator requesting changes; approval snapshots terms, point cost and each schedule's capacity.

## Visitor reservations

- Only verified visitor accounts in an enabled UK city can reserve an offer available in their saved city.
- The visitor confirms the point cost and cancellation terms before booking. A database transaction locks the offer, visitor balance and daily quota, enforces the total offer limit, and creates one unique voucher. Branch vouchers deduct points once at reservation; cancellation refunds the same amount. Online offers do not create vouchers or point transactions.
- A visitor may reserve the **same discount offer up to three times during its entire duration**, without a waiting period, including on the same date. Each successful reservation consumes a voucher and any applicable customer points. Cancelled reservations do not count toward three; redeemed and expired reservations still count.
- Free-product and free-meal benefits for the **same product are limited to once every seven rolling days**, measured from the successful reservation timestamp. This spans republished offers and both public and points rewards; cancelled reservations do not impose a cooldown. A durable product identity survives clearing the original product reference. Other benefit kinds retain one non-cancelled reservation per offer per UK date.
- Retrying the same reservation key returns the original voucher, including after cancellation, without creating another voucher or charging again. Rebooking after cancellation requires a new reservation key.
- Allocation is first successful reservation, not a seller-selected shortlist: a total capacity of 20 permits 20 non-cancelled reservations across all cities and dates, not necessarily 20 distinct people. Daily limits and eligible time windows still apply. Cancelling releases a place; redeemed and expired unused vouchers still count toward the total.
- There is no cooldown between different products or different discount offers. The same-product free-benefit rule above is the exception. These limits apply to RonTab branch vouchers, not untracked external website codes.
- Each branch offer shows an accessible reserved/total progress bar and remaining quantity. One batched anonymous aggregate request refreshes visible availability every 10 seconds and on tab focus, skipping hidden tabs. Changed capacity refreshes the server-rendered time slots, so cancellation can reopen a full offer. The eligibility deadline also refreshes a blocked free benefit. Full or ended offers cannot be submitted; the database enforces capacity and expiry atomically even between UI updates.
- Booking limits require `20261019230000_offer_booking_limits_and_capacity.sql`; audience and public voucher pricing require `20261019250000_public_offer_points_cost.sql`. If the private eligibility RPC is unavailable, new branch reservations are not offered by the gallery. The RPC exposes only the current eligible visitor's counts and next free-benefit date, never another visitor's history.
- Reservations atomically check the point cost confirmed by the visitor under the same offer lock used for administrator repricing. A changed quote is rejected without spending points and asks the customer to review the new price. Retrying a successful reservation retains its original voucher even after repricing.
- Vouchers cannot be transferred. A visitor can cancel before the scheduled offer window starts; the system then restores the points and releases the quota. Cancellation is closed once the window starts.
- Unused vouchers expire at the end of their scheduled window without a refund. Expired vouchers still count toward personal limits and shared capacity; early cancellation returns points, shared capacity and the cancelled reservation's personal allowance.
- An active seller checks a voucher code without changing its state, then confirms handover to mark it used. Only their own offer can be redeemed, during its UK-local window, once; redemption never deducts points again.
- Explicitly cancelling/ending an offer through seller or administrator controls cancels unused reserved vouchers whose windows have not ended and refunds each voucher's original point cost once, including during an already-started window. Already redeemed or expired vouchers are not refunded. Repeated seller cancellation is idempotent. Pausing an offer is different and preserves reservations.
- A visitor may report one issue for a voucher that can no longer be cancelled. Administrators review the case and record a resolution note; reporting or resolving a case never refunds or changes points automatically.

## Seller audience privacy

Audience counts use verified eligible visitor profiles in each audience city. Counts below five are withheld; missing counts are shown as unavailable, never fabricated as zero. Visible counts are aggregates only. Profile city is self-reported and is not proof of residence.

## Media and operations

Uploaded offer media is stored in a private Supabase bucket. Sellers can read and remove media only for editable requests; administrators can review submitted media, and visitors can read media only for published offers. The gallery and review page use short-lived signed URLs.

Product offers retain a durable `product_media_path` rather than storing an expiring signed URL. Media referenced by published/paused offers cannot be deleted through authenticated Storage access. Deleting a product clears the product reference without deleting the offer snapshot. Media failures use a placeholder rather than breaking reservations.

Apply repository migrations to a local database for testing. Do not apply these migrations to the linked production database without explicit approval. Run `npm run test:offer-concurrency` with the local Supabase stack running for the last daily place; add `-- --total-capacity` for different dates, `-- --total-capacity --free` for zero-cost contention, or `-- --same-request` for simultaneous idempotent retries. SQL smoke files under `supabase/tests` can be run directly through local `psql`; the rollback-only `product_public_offers_and_rewards.sql` covers audience separation, points deduction, refunds, publication, ownership, non-consuming inspection, total caps and idempotency.

`npm run test:product-offers` checks administrator point policy, customer-only wording, form modes, All UK and multi-city selection, admin/server validation, profile deep links and city filtering before result limits. `npm run test:offer-concurrency -- --multi-city` checks the last daily place across different cities; add `--free` for zero-cost reservations. SQL smoke tests cover future-city All UK eligibility, twenty-place allocation, immediate bookings on another offer, stale-price rejection, original-price refunds, multi-city quotas and publication retries. `node scripts/test-product-offers.mjs --preview` serves isolated, read-only component fixtures on port 3002 for responsive layout inspection; this is not an authenticated end-to-end test.

Booking-limit checks reuse these suites: `npm run test:offer-concurrency -- --discount-limit` races the third and fourth booking; `-- --free-product` races two offers for the same free product and visitor. SQL assertions cover cross-date discount limits, exact seven-day eligibility, cancellation reopening, private counts and durable product identity. Component checks cover full/ended/reopened capacity, aggregate endpoint validation, one visibility-aware polling lifecycle and cooldown-deadline refresh. The `/capacity` preview fixture shows available, full and reopened cards.

## Seller suspension

- Suspending or deactivating a seller automatically pauses that seller's published offers and records each pause in the admin audit log. Paused offers are hidden from visitors and cannot receive new bookings.
- Existing vouchers are preserved, not automatically cancelled or refunded. A suspended seller cannot redeem them while inactive. If the seller is reactivated before a voucher window ends, the seller may redeem an existing voucher against its paused offer; reactivation does not republish the offer or accept new reservations.
- Vouchers cancelled before their window still follow the standard visitor refund rule. Vouchers that expire remain non-refundable under the current published terms; administrators must handle exceptional disputes through support and must not alter balances directly.
- Voucher issue reports appear in the seller-offer admin queue and must be resolved with an administrative note. Reports do not compensate points automatically; explicit offer cancellation is the automatic refund path for unused active reservations.
