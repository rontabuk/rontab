# Creator Programme

## Application and review

Creators register a **standalone creator account** at `/creator/register`, exactly like sellers have their own accounts. Registration collects an email address, password, country and city, an optional phone number, a required profile picture, and the creator profile itself: a display name, introduction, one to five specialties, up to ten optional audience cities, links to social platforms, and up to three work samples. Audience cities describe where followers are based, not the creator's home. The creator must explicitly consent to publishing these fields and must report at least 3,000 followers in total across their platforms; the database rejects applications below that threshold. The account works immediately, but a confirmation link is emailed at registration (and can be resent from the studio); the database refuses to approve or republish a creator profile until the owner's email is confirmed (`profiles.email_verified_at`), and the admin review queue shows each applicant's confirmation status.

A creator account is not linked to a visitor or seller account. It has its own sign-in (`/creator/login`), its own studio (`/creator`), its own profile picture, and only one balance: Rontab influence. Creator accounts never earn or spend visitor loyalty points, cannot follow creators as fans, and are excluded from visitor offers and reward notifications. Someone who also wants to shop as a visitor or sell as a seller must use a different email address for each account. Accounts are marked with `app_metadata.account_type = 'creator'`, which only the service role can set during registration; `is_creator_account(uuid)` recognises that marker or ownership of a creator profile. Registration is atomic: if any step fails, the new auth user and uploaded media are removed.

Ordinary visitors see only a **Become a creator** button. It opens a dialog explaining that applications need at least 3,000 social followers and that choosing "Not ready yet" does not reduce future chances; "Yes, I have" continues to the separate creator registration. Creator images remain private while submitted; administrators can review them, and only approved image paths are available to the public. Changing approved images requires resubmitting the full creator profile; the current public images remain visible until the update is approved.

Applications move through `submitted`, `changes_requested`, `approved`, `rejected`, `suspended`, and `withdrawn` states. Only public profiles appear in the directory. Review notes are visible to the creator and administrators, not to the public. A creator who changes an approved profile submits a private draft for review; the last approved profile remains public until the new version is approved. A rejection or change request does not remove that approved version. Creators can take their profile offline and submit it again later with fresh public consent. Administrators can reinstate a suspended profile to the status it had before suspension.

## Public information and metrics

Approved profiles show the creator's profile picture, cover image, display name, introduction, specialties, audience cities, platform handles and links, follower counts, and up to three portfolio links. Creators may save a phone number and choose public visibility or collaboration-only visibility; collaboration-only is the default and is never shown to ordinary visitors. Public phone numbers appear only on the creator profile after explicit opt-in and a confirmed account email. The counts are self-reported and are not verified by Rontab. Platform links must use HTTPS and the matching platform domain. Email addresses, account ownership identifiers, and pending edits are not public profile fields.

## Creator collaborations

Product promotion opportunities are the only seller–creator collaboration workflow. The earlier direct "private collaboration request" workflow (free-text compensation, accept/decline) was removed by `20261020100000_creator_opportunity_pricing_completion_reviews.sql`, which drops its table, RPCs, triggers and notification events. Creators still receive durable notifications for review decisions, suspension and reinstatement.

### Product promotion opportunities

Active sellers with a public shop profile can open one promotion opportunity for an approved product, set a visible budget of at least £20, choose whether creators may propose a different price, select creator specialties and either the whole United Kingdom or up to fifteen target cities, describe the requested content, set an optional deadline, and indicate whether they will provide the product. The public shop profile lists the shop name, description, location, website, and approved products. Only approved creators matching the specialties and at least one selected audience city can see a city-targeted opportunity. Nationwide opportunities can be seen by matching creators without a listed audience city.

Creators can submit one pending offer per opportunity. When the seller allows counter-offers (the default) the price may be above or below the budget; when the seller marks the promotion as fixed price, the offer must equal the budget. The offer includes delivery details and explicit email and/or phone sharing preferences. They can withdraw a pending offer and submit again. Sellers can compare offers by amount, self-reported social follower totals, and audience city, then select one creator or close the opportunity. Selection marks the remaining pending offers as not selected and reveals only the contact methods both parties chose to share. Seller email and phone must be confirmed before they can be shared; creator email must be confirmed. Creator phone numbers are private and visible only after offer selection.

Sellers can also invite one specific approved creator from that creator's public profile (`/creators/<slug>`). An invitation is a private opportunity: only the invited creator can see it and is notified with a direct link, it inherits the creator's published specialties, and a seller may hold at most one open invitation per product and creator alongside one open public opportunity per product.

After selection the collaboration follows `open → awarded → delivered → completed` (or `closed`). The selected creator is notified and submits an HTTPS link to the published content with an optional note. The seller reviews the delivery and either confirms completion or requests changes with a 10–1,000 character note, which returns the collaboration to the creator. Each step sends a durable notification to the other party. Once completed, the seller and the creator can each leave one 1–5 rating with an optional comment. Ratings are readable by the two participants and administrators; public aggregates (average and count) are exposed through `get_creator_opportunity_ratings(uuid[])` and `get_seller_opportunity_ratings(uuid[])` and shown on creator profiles, seller proposal lists and creator opportunity pages.

Rontab does not collect, hold, release, or guarantee campaign payments. The seller and selected creator arrange payment and delivery directly. Seller phone numbers default to collaboration-only and are shared with the selected creator only when chosen for that opportunity. Sellers may separately opt to show a confirmed phone on public product detail pages. Opportunity matching uses approved product and creator snapshots; updating the creator's audience cities remains private until the profile update is approved.

## Fan codes and attribution

Each approved creator receives a unique `RT-` code. A confirmed visitor can follow multiple creators; the `(visitor, creator)` relationship is unique, so re-entering a code does not inflate the fan count. The first accepted code is stored separately as the visitor's original creator referral. Later codes can add follows but do not replace that attribution. Entering a code gives the visitor no loyalty points and does not automatically grant promotion credits to the creator.


## Rontab influence

Influence points are the creator account's only balance: a non-spendable reputation metric, not a balance transfer or an admin grant. One point represents one qualified original referral from `creator_first_referrals`. The visitor must have a confirmed auth email, actual inbox verification in `profiles.email_verified_at`, an enabled supported country, and at least one `visit_earned` transaction at or after the original code attribution. Banned/deleted accounts, administrators, sellers, creator accounts, and the creator's own account are excluded. Multiple product visits never multiply one visitor's influence contribution. The separate 30-day metric counts qualified referrals with earned product-visit activity in the last 30 days, still after attribution.

Raw fan follows and original referrals remain separate counts. Following multiple creators, repeatedly entering a code, or unfollowing cannot duplicate or transfer original referral credit. Existing referrals qualify automatically when these criteria are met; no historical balances are rewritten. Metrics are computed on read, so verification and account eligibility changes take effect on the next page request. Visitor loyalty points remain unchanged. A visitor can earn personal points and independently qualify their original creator's influence without transferring or spending those personal points.

| Qualified referrals | Influence badge |
| --- | --- |
| 0-99 | No earned badge |
| 100-499 | Bronze star |
| 500-999 | Silver star |
| 1,000-4,999 | Gold star |
| 5,000-9,999 | Silver shield |
| 10,000+ | Gold shield |

Profile approval keeps its neutral badge and never changes colour based on follower counts. Influence stars/shields are separate from identity verification. Displayed influence includes the qualified count and, in detailed views, raw fans, first referrals, and recent active referrals. The studio includes progress to the next level. Public profiles, the creator directory, seller proposals, followed creators, and admin creator review use the same thresholds. Sellers can sort proposals or the directory by influence or recent active referrals; this does not override campaign eligibility, location/specialty matching, price, or the seller's selection. Social follower counts remain explicitly self-reported. Downloaded certificates are dated snapshots with the live profile URL, not signed proof.

The first accepted code establishes attribution; it is not proof of a new signup or a conversion from a particular external platform. Existing users may enter their first code, and recent activity is not proof of incremental sales. Email/activity checks and uniqueness reduce simple inflation but do not prove unique people or replace dedicated fraud monitoring. No device/IP tracking or automated external-platform verification is introduced.

`get_creator_influence(uuid[])` accepts at most 200 profile IDs and returns only aggregate counts. Public creators are visible to everyone; unpublished metrics are limited to the owner and administrators. Fan identities remain private. The server batches/deduplicates requests and treats an undeployed RPC (`PGRST202`) as unavailable, not as zero earned influence. Apply `20261018220000_creator_influence.sql` before expecting live influence counts; no remote database deployment is performed by the code change alone.

Validation: `npm run test:creator-influence`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, and `supabase/tests/creator_influence.sql` (a transaction that rolls back all fixture data) against a local database after applying the migration.
